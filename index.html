OnFoot Privacy Policy

Walk Safe, Walk Smart

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Welcome to OnFoot! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our pedestrian navigation and safety app.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile picture (optional)

1.2 Location Data

Important: Location data is essential for OnFoot to function. We collect your location to:

  • Provide walking navigation
  • Show nearby safety reports
  • Allow you to report safety issues at your current location

1.3 Safety Reports

When you create a safety report, we store:

  • Report type (dangerous area, construction, poor lighting)
  • Location coordinates
  • Timestamp
  • Your user ID (to allow you to manage your reports)

1.4 Walks and Places

When you navigate with OnFoot while signed in, we store:

  • Start and destination coordinates of each walk, its distance, date and whether it was completed
  • Optional feedback you give about a route
  • The cities you have visited with the app

1.5 SOS Calls

When you use the SOS button, we record your location, country, the emergency number called and the time. This is linked to your account if you are signed in.

1.6 Usage Statistics

We record basic app events (for example, app opened or walk completed) with a random device identifier, your country, the app version and, if signed in, your user ID. We use this only to improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Navigation: To provide accurate walking directions
  • Safety Features: To display community-reported safety information
  • App Improvement: To understand how users interact with the app
  • Account Management: To maintain your profile and saved data

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data.

Your data may be shared in these limited circumstances:

  • Safety Reports: Report locations are visible to all users (without personal identification)
  • Service Providers: We use Supabase for secure data storage, Mapbox for maps and Google Maps Platform for place search and routes
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect safety

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Supabase: Cloud database with encryption at rest
  • HTTPS: All data transmitted is encrypted
  • Row Level Security: You can only access and delete your own data

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data associated with your account
  • Delete: Remove your safety reports at any time
  • Account Deletion: Delete your account and data at any time (see section 7)
  • Opt-out: Disable location services (note: this will limit app functionality)

6. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account
  • Safety reports may be automatically removed after a period of time to keep information current
  • Walk history, visited cities, SOS records and usage events linked to your account are kept until you delete your account

7. How to Delete Your Account

In the app: open your Profile and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app: email support@lotiam.dev from the email address of your OnFoot account with the subject "Delete my OnFoot account". We will delete it within 30 days.

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete:

  • Your account (email, name, profile picture)
  • Your walk history and visited cities
  • Your SOS records
  • Usage events linked to your account

Your safety reports stay on the map anonymously, with no link to you, because they help other pedestrians stay safe. If you want them removed as well, delete them in the app before deleting your account, or mention it in your email.

8. Children's Privacy

OnFoot is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

OnFoot uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Mapbox: For maps and navigation (Mapbox Privacy Policy)
  • Google Maps Platform: For place search and walking routes (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: For data storage (Supabase Privacy Policy)

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email.

11. Contact Us

OnFoot is part of the Lotiam ecosystem. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us and include "OnFoot" in the subject of your email:
